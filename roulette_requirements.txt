Create a simulation of a roulette strategy in the same vein as the current three simulation.html files.

Start with a $1000 bankroll baseline.

Strategy:
1. Always bet on 7 of the 36 numbers. (not 0 or 00)
2. Start betting $1 per number. If you lose 5 spins in a row, up the bet to $2 per number. If you
lose another 5 spins in a row, up the bet to $3 and so on.
3. If you win a spin, ratchet back the bet escalation 5 spins. For example, if you were on the 4th spin of
betting $3, go back to being on the 4th spin of betting $2.
4. If you any point you push above your baseline bankroll, immediately reset the escalation
to the first spin at $1/number. Every high water mark becomes a new baseline.
